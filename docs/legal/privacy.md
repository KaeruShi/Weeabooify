# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how **Weeabooify** collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

## 1. Information We Collect

The data collected depends on the version you use:

| Data | Trial Version | Paid Version |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| Device ID | ✅ | ✅ |
| Location (foreground & background) | ✅ | ✅ |
| Application logs | ✅ | ✅ |
| Email address | ❌ | ✅ |

Location data and Device ID are used to prevent Trial Version abuse and for app optimization. Email is used to verify your premium license.

## 2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used for:

- Verifying your license and preventing Trial version abuse
- Service optimization and debugging
- Security and piracy prevention
- Personalized feature support
- Analytics and usage reports

## 3. Data Storage & Retention

- All collected data is stored securely on private servers.
- Data is **never sold or shared** with third parties outside the services described in this policy.
- Data is retained as long as your account is active.

## 4. Root Access

The app requires root access to function. Root access is **not used** to collect data outside the scope of this Privacy Policy. By granting root access, you acknowledge the associated risks.

## 5. Data Security

Appropriate security measures are implemented to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

## 6. Your Rights

- You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting support.
- **Note for Paid users:** Deleting your data/account will permanently disable your premium license.
